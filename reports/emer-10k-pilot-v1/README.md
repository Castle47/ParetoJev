## 解释边界

两项要求比较同时通过：是。不因本结果追加样本、改模型或改阈值。
本轮打开了原5万组中的固定1万组；后续正式确认必须明确排除这些ID，不能再把完整5万组称为未见测试。
全部模型仍是此前冻结的候选；没有新训练。条件区间仅描述固定模型预测，不覆盖完整训练方差，也不是线上因果收益。

This 10000-slate pilot opens part of the previously untouched 50000-slate cohort; it is exploratory and does not replace a full heldout confirmation.
The remaining 40000 slates stay unevaluated and must exclude these pilot IDs in any later formal confirmation.
Logged-exposure pseudo-slates are not an online randomized experiment.
Point guards do not prove simultaneous population improvement on every goal.
No result-dependent sample extension, model change, threshold change, or pilot-ID replacement is allowed.
